# BrowserOnly: Product Hunt launch copy

## Product name

BrowserOnly

## Tagline

AI that acts in your tabs and helps you think through them

## Short description

Use your existing Chrome tabs to get things done or think through what you find. BrowserOnly's Operator mode works through websites in your signed-in browser session. Ask mode helps you explore questions through book-inspired and expert-style analysis. The extension is open source, and you choose the AI provider.

## Launch page description

BrowserOnly brings an AI assistant into Chrome's side panel. In Operator mode, describe a task in plain language and let it navigate sites, work across tabs, fill forms, read PDFs, or extract data using your existing browser session.

When you want to think rather than act, switch to Ask mode. Choose a book's ideas to discuss a situation, or choose an expert-inspired thinking framework for a structured explanation, comparison, or critique. Expert conversations can use the current tab as context or run standalone. Book conversations are standalone. Ask mode focuses on the conversation and does not operate the page.

You can also translate pages, save a task to run again, and monitor page text, prices, or stock indicators for changes. Saved workflows support manual runs and local schedules. Monitors check pages without calling an AI model.

BrowserOnly is open source and runs without a BrowserOnly backend. Configure a supported AI provider in the extension, including Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Ollama, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Content sent to your chosen provider is subject to that provider's policies. For actions such as purchases or public posts, BrowserOnly can pause for your approval.

## First maker comment

Hi Product Hunt! We built BrowserOnly because we wanted an assistant that could work in the browser we already use. Operator mode can read a page, compare information across tabs, fill out a form, or repeat a task later from Chrome's side panel. Ask mode gives you a separate place to explore a question through a book's ideas or an expert-inspired framework, with the option to include the current tab in an expert conversation.

This release also includes page translation, PDF reading, structured extraction, local web monitors, and saved workflows. Privacy was a priority for us: BrowserOnly is open source, runs in your Chrome browser, and has no BrowserOnly backend between you and the AI provider you choose. Content needed for AI features is sent to that provider; your tabs, sessions, monitors, and saved workflows stay in your browser.

I'd love to hear what browser task you would try first, and where the assistant needs more control or clarity. If you test it on a site you use often, tell us what worked and what got in the way.

## Claims to keep precise

- BrowserOnly has no BrowserOnly backend, but AI requests go to the provider you configure.
- Scheduled workflows and monitors run locally and require Chrome and the computer to remain running.
- Approval is available for sensitive actions; do not imply that every action is automatically reviewed.
- Browser automation depends on the website and may be interrupted by login expiry, CAPTCHAs, or site changes.
